$$
A x = b
$$

- \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) is the sparse coefficient matrix,
- \(x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\) is the unknown solution vector,
- \(b \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\) is the right-hand-side vector.

$$
PAQ = LU
$$

$$
x = A^{-1}b
$$
